Dungeon maps for Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands,
City of Dalaran (with the Dalaran Sewers) and Blackmaw Hold (with the
Festering Grotto and its four levels below)

Map art by Santiago Reyes, from his Atlas de Azeroth: Forever series.
Used in NeedRoll with his permission. The art is his and is not covered by
NeedRoll's GNU GPL v3 license; please don't reuse it from here, ask him.
His watermark is kept on each map, which is only resized and cut into tiles
(the title banner below the City of Dalaran maps is cut off).

Dev/ holds maps by Santiago Reyes of dungeons NeedRoll doesn't show players
yet: The Drowned City (with Atal'Byzal), Krol'dok Stronghold, Shaper's
Terrace and Alcaz Prison (Main Unit and Maximum Security Unit). They are for
local development checkouts only and are left out of the released addon. The
same terms apply to them, and each is only resized and cut into tiles with
his watermark kept.
The character sketches on Alcaz Prison's Maximum Security Unit map are
original illustrations by Nysha (@nyswhf) and PallBadguy (@pall.bdguy), as
the map's corner credit says.

The maps of the Classic dungeons and raids are Blizzard's own world map art
(the Wrath-era drawings). Classic/ holds them, copied unchanged from the
game client's enUS files (interface/worldmap/<map>/, file names kept): the
Russian, Mexican Spanish and Italian clients don't have them, so NeedRoll
ships them rather than drawing them from the game's files. So are
UpperBlackrockSpire1_* and UpperBlackrockSpire2_*: Blizzard's drawings of
Upper Blackrock Spire's first two floors, which the game's files lack. This
art is Blizzard Entertainment's and is not covered by NeedRoll's GNU GPL v3
license.
